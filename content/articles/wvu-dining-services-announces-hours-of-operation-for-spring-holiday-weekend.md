      In observance of the spring holiday on Friday, April 18, WVU Dining
      Services will operate under a modified schedule through Sunday, April 20.
      Several dining locations, including those at Evansdale Crossing and the
      Health Sciences Center, will be closed.


      Normal hours of operation will resume on Monday, April 21.


      Open locations and hours include:


      * **Dining Halls (Café Evansdale, Hatfields and Summit Café):** 9 a.m. – 7
      p.m.

      * **Chick-fil-A (Mountainlair):** Noon – 8 p.m. (Friday and Saturday)

      * **Grab & Go (Mountainlair):** Noon – 8 p.m.

      * **Jazzmans (Mountainlair):** 9 a.m. – 7 p.m. (Friday only)

      * **Panda Express (Mountainlair):** Noon – 8 p.m. (Friday), 10:30 a.m. – 8
      p.m. (Saturday and Sunday)

      * **Which Wich (Mountainlair):** Noon – 8 p.m. (Friday only)

      * **Lyon’s Den (Towers):** 7 p.m. – 1 a.m.

      * **Park Place (University Park):** 7:30 a.m. – 7 p.m. (Friday), 11 a.m. –
      5 p.m. (Saturday and Sunday)

      * **The Market @ UPlace (including Starbucks):** 9 a.m. – 7 p.m.


      All other dining locations will be closed. To view the full schedule,
      visit
      [diningservices.wvu.edu/special-hours-of-operation](https://diningservices.wvu.edu/special-hours-of-operation).


      Follow @WVUDining on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/WVUdining/) and
      [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/wvudining/) for the latest news and
      information.
